To build from source, put these two jars here, renamed EXACTLY:

  monicraft-1.0.0.jar   (the Monicraft jar)
  tacz-1.0.0.jar        (the TACZ jar for Forge 1.20.1)

The "1.0.0" is only a label for Gradle; it does not need to match the real versions.
These jars belong to their authors and are not included in this repository.
